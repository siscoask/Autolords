# AUTOLORDS

**The Gear Master.**

AUTOLORDS is Engr. Jidey's transmission and gear-engineering practice — automatic transmission overhauling, custom gear design, and diagnostics, built on twenty-plus years split between the wrench and the CAD bench. This is his site.

> *"I work with gears. Not just transmissions — the whole system underneath them."*

---

## The idea

Most tradesman sites tell you what someone does. This one is Jidey talking — in his own voice, from the opening line to the booking form. There's no "About the Founder" written in the third person, no certifications wall, no stock photography. The site is built entirely from typography, an interactive gear diagram, and a small set of custom notation marks that repeat through every section until they read as a signature.

The six section marks — `↻ ♀ △ ♄ → +` — aren't decoration. Each one belongs to exactly one part of the story:

| Mark | Section |
|------|---------|
| `A.01 ↻` | **I work with gears** — the opening statement, alongside a real, interactive planetary-gear diagram (hover or tap the ring, the planets, or the sun gear to see how each part works) |
| `A.02 ♀` | **In plain terms** — what a customer actually needs to know, translated out of engineering language |
| `A.03 △` | **This is how I work** — Diagnose → Model → Machine → Assemble → Test, as one connected sequence |
| `A.04 ♄` | **The bench** — SolidWorks, CNC, Bosch diagnostics, CAD/CAM, in his own words |
| `A.05 →` | **What I've built** — four case studies from the shop, archive-style, each with its own annotated diagram |
| `A.06 +` | **Ready to look at yours?** — booking, straight to WhatsApp |

No photography anywhere on the site — every visual is a drawn diagram, by design.
